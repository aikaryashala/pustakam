Unit IV · Presentations with AI

16. Slides with Gamma AI

Gamma is a tool that makes a full presentation from a few lines of text. You give the topic. It writes the slides, chooses a design and adds images. This is Lab experiment 13.

Link: gamma.app. Sign up with Gmail. The free plan gives you credits to make several presentations.

Step by step

  1. Open Gamma. Click Create new.
  2. Choose Generate. (Other options: Paste in text if you already have notes, or Import file for a Word or PDF file.)
  3. Choose Presentation.
  4. Type your topic. Be specific. Example:
prompt
Solar energy for a college campus in Visakhapatnam: how it works, costs, benefits, a simple plan for our college. For first year engineering students. 8 slides.
  1. Choose the number of slides and the language. Click Continue.
  2. Gamma shows an outline. Read it. Edit it. Delete or add points. Click Continue.
  3. Choose a theme (colours and fonts). Click Generate.
  4. Wait a minute. Your presentation is ready.

Editing the slides

  • Click any text to edit it.
  • Click an image, then Replace to use another image. Gamma can also generate a new AI image from a prompt.
  • Click the + between slides to add a slide.
  • Use the right side panel for Theme, Layout and Images.
  • Ask the AI helper (the sparkle icon) things like "Make this slide shorter" or "Add a slide about costs".

Exporting

Click Share at the top, then Export.

  • PowerPoint (PPTX) — to open in MS PowerPoint or Google Slides. Needed for the lab.
  • PDF — for printing or emailing.
  • You can also Present directly from the browser.

Making it good

Gamma makes a decent first draft. Make it yours:

  • Check every fact. Gamma can hallucinate numbers.
  • Replace generic images with real ones. A photo of your own campus is better than a stock image.
  • Keep one idea per slide. Delete slides that say nothing new.
  • The first slide should have a clear title and your name, roll number and branch.
  • The last slide should be a summary or "Thank you" with a question for the audience.

Other AI presentation tools

  • Canva Presentations — Magic Design for presentations.
  • Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint — needs a paid plan.
  • Google Slides with Gemini — covered in the next chapter.
  • Beautiful.ai, Tome, Pitch — similar to Gamma.

Try it

  • Make an 8-slide presentation on an engineering topic from your branch. Examples: "How a lithium-ion battery works", "Smart traffic lights for Vizag", "3D printing in mechanical engineering", "How the Vizag port handles containers".
  • Export it as PPTX. Open it in Google Slides and check that nothing broke.
  • Try Paste in text: paste the summary you made in Chapter 9 and let Gamma turn it into slides.

New words

  • Presentation — a set of slides shown to an audience; also called a PPT or a deck.
  • Slide — one page of a presentation.
  • Outline — the list of slide titles and main points, made before the slides.
  • Theme — the colours, fonts and style of a presentation.
  • Layout — how text and images are arranged on a slide.
  • Export — to save in a different file format, like PPTX or PDF.
  • PPTX — the file format of Microsoft PowerPoint.
  • Credits — points on a free plan; each AI generation uses some.
  • Stock image — a general-purpose photo from an image library.
