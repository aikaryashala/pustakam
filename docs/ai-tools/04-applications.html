Unit I · Introduction to AI Tools

4. Where Generative AI is used

Generative AI is already used in many fields. Here are the main ones. Think about which ones matter for your branch.

Education

  • Explaining a hard topic in simple words, in English or Telugu.
  • Making practice questions before an exam.
  • Summarising a long chapter into one page.
  • Acting as a patient tutor at 2 am.

Writing and office work

  • Writing emails, letters, notices and reports.
  • Fixing grammar and spelling.
  • Translating between languages.
  • Making slides for a presentation.

Software and engineering

  • Writing code in C, Python, Java and more.
  • Finding and fixing bugs.
  • Writing test cases and documentation.
  • Explaining old code that someone else wrote.

Design and media

  • Making images, logos and posters from text.
  • Making greeting cards and social media posts.
  • Generating voice-overs and background music.
  • Making short videos.

Healthcare

  • Writing a summary of a patient's history for a doctor.
  • Helping to read X-rays and scans (with a doctor checking).
  • Answering common health questions in local languages.

Agriculture

  • A farmer in Anakapalle can take a photo of a sick plant and ask what is wrong.
  • Advice on weather, crops and prices in Telugu.
  • Government schemes explained in simple words.

Business and marketing

  • Writing product descriptions and advertisements.
  • Chatbots that answer customer questions 24 hours a day.
  • Studying customer reviews to find common complaints.

Science and research

  • Reading hundreds of research papers and giving a summary.
  • Suggesting new molecules for medicines.
  • Helping to write and check research papers.

Try it

  • Ask Gemini: "How can a civil engineer in Visakhapatnam use Generative AI? Give 5 examples." Change "civil" to your branch.
  • Open Google Translate and translate a paragraph from English to Telugu. Then ask Gemini to do the same. Compare.
  • In WhatsApp, open Meta AI and ask it to make an image of "the Vizag lighthouse at night".

New words

  • Application — a use of something for a real purpose; also a software program (app).
  • Summarise — to say the main points in fewer words.
  • Documentation — written notes that explain how a program or product works.
  • Chatbot — a program you can talk to by typing or speaking.
  • Voice-over — a recorded voice that speaks over a video or slides.
  • Scheme — a plan or program, often from the government.
