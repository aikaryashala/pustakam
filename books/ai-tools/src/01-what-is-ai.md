# What is AI?

## A simple definition

**AI** means **Artificial Intelligence**. *Artificial* means made by humans. *Intelligence* means the ability to learn, understand and solve problems.

So AI is a computer program that can do things which normally need human intelligence. For example:

- Understanding your voice when you say "Ok Google".
- Finding your friend's face in a photo.
- Suggesting the next video on YouTube.
- Finding the fastest route from Gajuwaka to RK Beach on Google Maps.
- Writing an email when you give it a few words.

A normal program follows fixed rules written by a programmer. An AI program **learns** from examples. This is the big difference.

## A short background

AI is not new. Here is a very short history.

- **1950:** Alan Turing asked, "Can machines think?" He suggested a test. If a machine can chat like a human, we can call it intelligent.
- **1956:** The name "Artificial Intelligence" was used for the first time at a meeting in Dartmouth, USA.
- **1960s to 1980s:** Early AI used rules written by experts. It worked for small problems. It failed for big ones. People lost interest. This time is called the "AI winter".
- **1997:** IBM's Deep Blue computer beat the world chess champion, Garry Kasparov.
- **2012:** A new method called **deep learning** became very good at seeing images. AI started to grow fast.
- **2017:** Google researchers made the **Transformer**. This is the design used by ChatGPT and Gemini today.
- **2022:** ChatGPT was released. In two months, 100 million people used it. This started the Generative AI wave.
- **Today:** AI can write, draw, talk, code and even make videos.

## Three kinds of AI

| Kind | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow AI | Does one job well | Google Maps, spam filter, face unlock |
| Generative AI | Creates new text, images, audio or code | ChatGPT, Gemini |
| General AI (AGI) | Can do any task a human can do | Does not exist yet |

All the AI we use today is narrow AI or generative AI. AGI is still a dream.

## Why should an engineer learn AI tools?

You will use AI tools in every branch. A civil engineer can ask AI to check a report. A mechanical engineer can ask AI to write a Python script to read sensor data. A chemical engineer can ask AI to explain a reaction. AI tools are like a calculator. They do not replace you. They make you faster.

> Think of AI as a smart helper. It is fast and knows many things. But it can make mistakes. You are the engineer. You must check its work.

## New words

- **Artificial** — made by humans, not natural.
- **Intelligence** — the ability to learn, understand and solve problems.
- **Program** — a set of instructions that a computer follows.
- **Rule** — a fixed instruction like "if X then do Y".
- **Deep learning** — a way for computers to learn from a lot of examples, using many layers of maths.
- **Transformer** — the design (architecture) used inside ChatGPT and Gemini.
- **Narrow AI** — AI that does only one kind of job.
- **AGI** — Artificial General Intelligence; an AI that can do anything a human can. Not real yet.
