# The tools: ChatGPT, Gemini, NotebookLM, AI Studio

This course uses four main tools. All of them are free to start. Make your accounts now.

## ChatGPT

**Made by:** OpenAI. **Link:** [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com)

ChatGPT is the most famous AI chat tool. You type a question. It answers. You can continue the chat, and it remembers what you said earlier in the same chat.

**Good for:** writing, explaining, coding, brainstorming, images.

**How to start:** Go to the link. Sign up with your Gmail. Type in the box at the bottom. Press Enter.

**Useful features:**

- Click the **+** button to upload a file or image.
- Use the microphone icon to speak instead of typing.
- Start a **new chat** for a new topic. Old chats are saved on the left side.

## Gemini

**Made by:** Google. **Link:** [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)

Gemini is Google's AI chat tool. It works like ChatGPT. It is built into many Google products: Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Android phones.

**Good for:** everything ChatGPT does, plus working with Google Docs, Drive and YouTube. It is strong in Indian languages, including Telugu.

**Useful features:**

- **Gems** — you can make your own custom helper with a fixed set of instructions. For example, a "C Lab Helper" Gem.
- **Deep Research** — Gemini reads many web pages and writes a long report.
- Can look at YouTube videos and summarise them.
- Can make images and short videos.

## NotebookLM

**Made by:** Google. **Link:** [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com)

NotebookLM is different. It does not answer from the internet. It answers only from the **documents you upload**. These are called **sources**. You can upload PDFs, Google Docs, websites, YouTube links and audio.

**Good for:** studying your textbooks and notes. It does not hallucinate much, because every answer points back to your source.

**Useful features:**

- Ask questions about your uploaded PDF. Every answer shows which page it came from.
- **Audio Overview** — makes a podcast where two voices discuss your document. Great for revising on the bus.
- Makes a study guide, FAQ, timeline or mind map in one click.

## Google AI Studio

**Made by:** Google. **Link:** [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com)

Google AI Studio is for people who want to see **how the model works under the hood**. It is the developer's view of Gemini. Gemini the app is like a car. AI Studio is like the engine with the bonnet open.

**Good for:** experimenting, building a small AI app, getting an API key.

**Useful features:**

- Choose different Gemini models and compare them.
- Set a **system instruction**. This is a rule the model always follows, like "Always answer in Telugu".
- Change the **temperature**. Low = more exact. High = more creative.
- The **Build** section can create a small web app from your description.
- Get a free **API key** to use Gemini from your own Python program.

## Comparing the four

| Tool | Answers from | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Its training + web search | General chat, writing, code |
| Gemini | Its training + Google search + your Google apps | General chat, Google Docs, Indian languages |
| NotebookLM | Only your uploaded files | Study, summaries, questions about your notes |
| AI Studio | Gemini models, with your settings | Experiments, building apps, API keys |

## Try it

This is **Lab experiment 1**. Ask the same question in all four tools and compare.

```prompt
Explain how a lithium-ion battery works, for a first year engineering student. Use simple English. Give 5 bullet points.
```

- Open ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Studio. Paste the prompt. Save all three answers in a document.
- Open NotebookLM. Add a Wikipedia page about lithium-ion batteries as a source. Ask the same question.
- Which answer was the simplest? Which was the most correct? Which one showed sources?

## New words

- **Account** — your personal login for a website.
- **Chat** — a conversation; in AI tools, one conversation is saved as one chat.
- **Upload** — to send a file from your computer to a website.
- **Source** — a document that an answer comes from.
- **Custom** — made for one specific purpose.
- **System instruction** — a rule given to the AI before the chat starts.
- **Temperature** — a setting that controls how random or creative the AI answers are.
- **API key** — a secret code that lets your own program use an AI service.
- **Under the hood** — the inside workings of something (like under the bonnet of a car).
